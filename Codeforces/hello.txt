Once upon a to, a small bird named Mira lived near a quiet forest. Every morning, she watched the larger birds fly high above the trees. Mira wanted to fly as high as they did, but she was afraid of falling.

One day, a strong wind shook the branches of her nest. Mira spread her wings and jumped. At first, she flew only a little distance. Then she tried again the next day, and the next.

Slowly, Mira became stronger. Soon, she was flying above the tallest trees, looking at the whole forest below.

Mira learned that courage does not mean having no fear. It means trying even when you are afraid.
